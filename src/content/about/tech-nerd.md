My fascination with technology predates medical school by quite a few years.

I've always followed technological developments, not necessarily because I understood every technical detail, but because I found the speed of progress fascinating.

For a long time, there was a gap between what I could imagine and what I could actually build. I wasn't a programmer, and learning to code never quite became something I pursued seriously.

Then artificial intelligence changed the equation.

Suddenly, many of the ideas that had previously existed only in my head became things I could experiment with, prototype, and eventually turn into working applications.

I'm particularly interested in web design, software development, and the practical applications of AI in healthcare.

I don't pretend to be a software engineer. I approach technology from the perspective of someone who understands certain real-world problems and enjoys exploring how digital tools might solve them.

There is still an enormous amount for me to learn, which is precisely what makes it exciting.
