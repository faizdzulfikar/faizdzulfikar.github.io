One of my earliest significant disappointments was not getting into the high school I wanted to attend. At the time, it felt like an enormous personal failure.

Looking back, it was simply one of many turns that led me somewhere I hadn't planned to go.

I've learned that progress rarely follows a perfectly straight line. Plans change, interests evolve, and sometimes the things that don't work out become part of a story that makes sense only much later.

I still have insecurities, unfinished projects, inconsistent habits, and more ambitions than I probably have time for.

I'm learning to make peace with that.

This website is not a finished portrait of who I am. It's a collection of my work, interests, experiments, thoughts, and experiences as they continue to evolve.

Part portfolio, part personal journal, part curiosity archive.\
And, much like everything else in my life, a work in progress.
