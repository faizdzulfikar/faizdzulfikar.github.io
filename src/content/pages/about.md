I trained in medicine at the Faculty of Medicine, Public Health and Nursing, Universitas Gadjah Mada, and graduated as a doctor in 2025. My internship took me first to Santa Elisabeth General Hospital, through its wards, emergency room and delivery suite, and then to Pundong Public Health Center, where I saw the same chronic diseases return month after month in the Prolanis clinic.

Those two settings shaped what I care about. The hospital showed me how much cardiovascular disease decides a patient's course. The puskesmas showed me that most of the work of preventing it happens long before anyone reaches a cath lab: in screening, follow-up, and records that are good enough to act on.

Today I work as a research assistant in the COHARD-PH division of the Department of Cardiology and Vascular Medicine at UGM and Dr. Sardjito Hospital. My long-term aim is cardiology. Before that, I want to study public health, so I can understand cardiovascular disease at the level of populations and health systems, not only individual patients.

## Building things

I'm not a software engineer. I know enough code to be dangerous and I build with AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT, which lets a clinician turn a workflow problem into a working app. Most of what I've made started as something I needed myself: a registry that didn't lose follow-ups, a Prolanis record that the puskesmas team could actually use, a quick reference for night shifts in the ER.

## Design and video

Before medicine took most of my time, I spent a lot of it on visual work. I've designed visual identities for MEDSKILL and VRIPE-Health, edited a public health magazine for CIMSA, and filmed and edited educational videos for Kolom Remaja. I still use Illustrator, Figma, Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro regularly.

## Away from work

I lift, I run, and I play tennis. Training is where I clear my head and the place I learn the most about consistency.
