On a busy ER shift, the answer you need is usually in a guideline you can't open fast enough. JagaIGD collects the algorithms, drug doses and checklists I reach for most often into an app that works in a few taps. It's inspired by emergency management courses like ALTEM, and it stays private because its content is written for my own practice.
