Patients with congenital heart disease and pulmonary hypertension manage a lot between clinic visits: several medications, fluid limits, home measurements, and results from different hospitals.

COHARD Patient puts that in one app:

- Medication regimens with a dose log
- Fluid intake and home measurements
- A personal health record with diagnoses, lab results, procedures and admissions
- Private document upload, with a full history of corrections

Planned next are daily reminders, a portable health passport, and a review workflow so registry staff can verify what patients enter.
