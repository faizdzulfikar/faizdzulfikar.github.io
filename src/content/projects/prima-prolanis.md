Prolanis is Indonesia's chronic disease management program for patients with hypertension and type 2 diabetes. At Pundong Public Health Center, patient data, lab results and control schedules lived in separate registers, which made follow-up slow and monthly reporting tedious.

PRIMA Prolanis brings it together:

- Patient search by name, NIK, BPJS number or medical record number
- Visit records with vital signs, ICD-10 diagnoses and ATC-coded prescriptions
- Serial lab results with charts over time
- Control schedules that flag overdue patients, with a list ready to copy into WhatsApp
- Hypertension and diabetes control indicators, calculated the same way every month
- Import from existing registers and lab reports, export to Excel and PDF
- Admin, operator and viewer roles, with an audit log of every change
